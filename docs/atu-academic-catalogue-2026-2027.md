## Decisions and discrepancies

- Qualification index rules remain HND=D, BTECH=B, MTECH=M. MSc, BSc and diploma index rules are not assumed.
- The directory lists 16 departments; the Applied Sciences faculty page additionally names Information Systems and Technology. Both are represented, with provenance.
- Distinct award titles are retained; morning/evening/weekend and top-up delivery variants are not separate qualifications. Automotive/Automobile and other naming variants are recorded as aliases.
- A null programme department means the department association was not verified. These choices are explicitly flagged for administrator confirmation, rather than assigned by guesswork.
- The 2026/2027 advertisement governs programme availability; current programme and department pages supplement aliases and associations. HND Dispensing Technology is explicitly listed by the SLT department.
- Department association for MTech Automotive/Plant Maintenance follows the Mechanical Engineering subject grouping; administrators confirm all selections before activation.
- Architectural Technology has a subject-based association with Building Technology, but the programme’s department needs explicit confirmation.
- Nine programme associations are flagged for administrator review. A null department is displayed as unconfirmed rather than assigned to an invented department.
- These academic selections are self-reported and saved separately from approved academic profiles. Registration remains pending. The administrator sees the request, confirms academic setup and then activates access.
- Existing accounts and academic records are retained. No bulk import or reassignment runs on public registration. Verified requested structure is added only after an administrator approves a request.
