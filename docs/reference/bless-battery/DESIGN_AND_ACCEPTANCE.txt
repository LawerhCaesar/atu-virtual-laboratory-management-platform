VISUAL AND INTERACTION CONTRACT - BLESS VIRTUAL LAB

Purpose: let a student, researcher or project reviewer configure a clear experiment,
understand energy flow, compare the two powertrains, and inspect the calculations'
results without navigating a Simulink diagram.

SCREEN ORGANIZATION

Desktop: a compact setup column at left; vehicle and energy-flow stage in the
center; electric/diesel comparison cards at right; synchronized charts and playback
controls below. Avoid a wall of gauges. Use clear units, restrained colors, generous
spacing and short labels. Place detailed calculations in an expandable expert view.
On a phone stack setup, stage, comparison and charts in that order; let tabs expose
analysis without shrinking labels. No horizontal page scrolling or clipped controls.

Suggested experiment groups:
  Driving: built-in cycle/constant speed, target speed, grade, duration, payload.
  Environment: ambient temperature and auxiliary electrical load.
  Power sources: fuel-cell enabled, initial hydrogen, initial battery SOC/temperature.
  Energy prices: electricity, delivered hydrogen, diesel; date and source notes.
  Advanced: vehicle masses, motor/converter ratings, efficiencies, full battery
            mask and OCV curve, fuel-cell control thresholds and thermal parameters.

Expose defaults from parameter_catalog.json, never invented defaults. The model's
main operating clamps include 0-100 km/h, -20% to +20% grade and 0-20 kW auxiliary
load; inspect the exact exported expressions and preserve them. Form validation
must show the accepted/effective value when the model clamps an input. Add sensible
validation for editable ratings: positive masses/capacity/resistance where required,
efficiencies in (0,1], positive integer series/parallel counts, matching OCV arrays,
exactly 13 matching OCV entries with SOC endpoints 0 and 100, strictly increasing
SOC breakpoints, nondecreasing OCV within voltage limits, ordered SOC/temperature thresholds, and
nonnegative fuel. Distinguish form rules from validated physical operating limits.
Do not silently reinterpret or recalibrate a rejected input.

Provide the ten test scenarios as labeled examples. Mark the price inputs in
priced examples 'Illustrative test prices'. The ordinary default retains unentered
prices. A scenario summary must state the chosen assumptions before Run.

MANDATORY VISUALS AND THEIR DATA BINDINGS

1. Passenger-van animation
   Side view with rotating wheels, a scrolling demonstration road and a subtle grade
   indicator. Wheel/road motion follows commanded speed, not a fabricated achieved
   speed. Caption: 'Requested-cycle playback'. Show the same time cursor used by
   every chart. At zero speed stop road and wheel motion. With unmet demand show a
   persistent amber/red 'Requested demand not supported' banner; never imply proven
   route completion. No geographic map or real route claim is required.

2. Energy-flow diagram
   Hydrogen -> fuel-cell module -> boost converter -> DC bus -> inverter -> motor.
   Battery <-> bidirectional converter <-> DC bus, with a separate regeneration
   path and a visible auxiliary-load branch. Highlight active paths from actual
   power signals. Reverse the battery arrow when charging; stop it near zero using
   a DISPLAY-only deadband. Show power in kW and the relevant loss/limit indicator.
   Braking demand and accepted regenerative power are different quantities.
   Do not animate unavailable hydrogen or an inactive fuel cell as generating power.
   Map each path to output_contract.json; add verified internal telemetry only when
   the existing outputs are insufficient, without changing the original equations.
   Physical power paths differ from Simulink information-signal connections.

3. State and status cards
   Battery SOC %, temperature deg C, terminal voltage V, current A (signed), usable
   hydrogen remaining kg, consumed hydrogen kg, and commanded speed km/h.
   Always display a text status as well as color. Separate current operating mode
   from the native cumulative comparison-eligibility flag. That flag does not
   directly test motor shortfall or the mass screen; show both independently.
   A low SOC/thermal limit must have a clear reason.

4. Comparison cards
   Commanded distance km; diesel used L and L/100 km; electric/diesel source energy
   kWh; energy saving %; refill cost GHS and cost saving GHS; feasibility/cost-valid
   indicators. Annotate the boundary: source energy excludes upstream production;
   refill cost excludes vehicle and infrastructure costs. Missing prices show
   'Enter prices', not zero cost or a misleading negative currency figure.
   The cost-SAVING comparison must not be shown as valid when cost_valid is false.
   Individual refill costs have the separate all-prices-entered gate. Do not
   conflate these two rules or hide the model's surplus tolerance of 0.001 kWh.

5. Synchronized charts (required, accessible legends and units)
   a. Commanded speed against time, with shortfall intervals highlighted.
   b. Battery SOC and hydrogen remaining, with separate units/axes or separate plots.
   c. Fuel-cell DC, signed battery DC and accepted regenerative power in kW.
   d. Battery temperature and current with their appropriate units.
   e. Cumulative electric and diesel source energy in kWh.
   f. Electric DC, motor and diesel shortfall in kW.
   g. Refill-cost comparison only where valid, with an unavailable explanation otherwise.
   A table exposes all 38 comparison channels, and an expert option exposes the
   electric, diesel and road vectors with the labels from the output contract.
   Exports contain original samples, not smoothed or screen-decimated values.

PLAYBACK AND ACCESSIBILITY

Run/recalculate, cancel calculation, play/pause playback, reset, one-second step,
time scrubber and playback-speed selector. Clearly distinguish calculation progress
from playback time. The engine's one-second calculation step never follows monitor
refresh rate. Do not interpolate Boolean validity flags or control-state transitions.
Screen readers must have numeric summaries and a table alternative. Every control
must work by keyboard with visible focus; use text/icons as well as color. Honor
prefers-reduced-motion and offer a manual motion toggle. Avoid flashing, constant
particle clutter and autoplay that blocks reading. Pause motion when the tab is
hidden while retaining the completed simulation data.

DEFAULT DEMONSTRATION AND EDGE-CASE STORYBOARD

Default: select built-in cycle -> Run -> watch requested-speed playback -> inspect
fuel-cell/battery sharing -> pause and scrub -> compare energy -> see that cost is
unavailable because default prices are not entered -> export the same result.

Overload: choose the 20% uphill test -> show positive shortfall -> make infeasibility
visible in the stage, charts and comparison -> do not advertise a valid saving.

Fuel exhaustion: choose the hydrogen-depletion test -> hydrogen reaches zero ->
fuel-cell flow stops -> battery/shortfall indicators follow the actual result.

Regeneration: choose the downhill/high-SOC test -> show the charge-direction flow
and accepted regeneration -> show limits without claiming all braking is recovered.

Prices: enter the illustrative priced test -> show costs with its source/date note
and valid flag -> export raw data and input snapshot. A genuine negative saving
remains visible when valid; the sentinel rule must not hide it.

VISUAL ACCEPTANCE DELIVERABLES

Deliver the actual integrated browser feature, not only a design file. Supply:
  - Desktop and narrow-phone screenshots of the final working default run.
  - A 20-40 second screen recording showing motion, power-flow changes, pause/scrub,
    and a correctly handled unavailable or infeasible result.
  - Default-run source-energy and power-flow time-series exports (SVG/PNG or PDF)
    generated from the converted engine, with units, legend, scenario and model version.
  - CSV/JSON result and scenario exports that agree with the visible values.
  - A numerical parity report for all ten scenarios/four streams, plus UI checks.

The product is complete only when the visual outputs are driven by the converted,
validated calculations and remain understandable in normal, constrained and invalid
comparison states. Optional 3D can be proposed after this baseline is delivered.
