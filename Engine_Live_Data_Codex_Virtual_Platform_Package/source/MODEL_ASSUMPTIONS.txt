MODEL ASSUMPTIONS AND LIMITS

1. This is an educational live-data relationship model, not a production ECU.
2. All student inputs are capped before ECU calculations.
3. Battery voltage is capped at 8.0–14.9 V.
4. The ECT voltage-to-temperature curve is a generic NTC-like teaching curve.
   Replace it with verified sensor calibration data when available.
5. The oxygen-voltage, lambda and STFT relationship is deliberately simplified
   to show direction and dependence. It is not OEM Toyota calibration data.
6. STFT means short-term fuel trim or the equivalent closed-loop correction
   available in a programmable ECU.
7. The virtual cooling model contains ECT1, ECT2, a PWM fan and an electric
   water pump. The physical 1NZ engine stage does not contain an electric pump.
8. The fingerprint and voice systems are represented in Simulink by an
   authorisation input and a start-request input. Their recognition algorithms
   are outside the virtual model.
9. The physical and virtual stages are tested independently; no comparison is
   required.
10. Do not use this model to command a road vehicle or safety-critical hardware.
