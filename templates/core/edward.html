Learning guide and validation boundaries

The motor drive connects a battery source, DC-link capacitor, inverter and PMSM. Buck, boost and rectifier experiments use independent sources and loads. The car illustration shows calculated shaft rotation and a kinematic wheel conversion; vehicle motion, SOC, road load and regeneration are outside this model.

Default operation approaches 1500 rpm. The load changes from 10 to 20 N m at 2 seconds. Compare changed operation at 1000 rpm, then inspect all six gates in the switched-PWM preset. Recorded samples and fine switching samples have separate CSV exports.

The supplied numerical suite includes 170 independent MATLAB checkpoints across three scenarios. The complete native binary traces were not supplied, so the historical comparison of 1,100,003 samples has not been repeated here. Parameters are educational model assumptions.
