    Requested force = vehicle mass × requested deceleration. Regeneration receives the smallest available demand, motor-torque, motor-power or battery-force allowance. Friction supplies the remainder. Battery acceptance combines current/power limits with separate SOC and temperature factors.

    Results remain fixed at the selected operating point during playback. SOC and temperature do not evolve, and recovered energy is not integrated. There is no road-load, ABS/ESC, tyre-grip, hydraulic, battery-electrochemical or thermal model. Stopping time/distance assume the requested constant deceleration; they are not validated stopping-performance predictions.

    Brake demand and SOC are normalised to their documented ranges and negative speed to zero, with warnings retaining the raw inputs. The protected force denominator (1 N by default, editable in Advanced calibration) means shares need not total 100% below that threshold; the original NO_BRAKING diagnostic also includes that region.

    Core logic uses the supplied guide and canonical prompt, not an inspected BrakeModel.slx. No MATLAB or physical-vehicle validation has been performed. Local JSON exports are learning evidence, not an assessment submission or a hardware-control command.
