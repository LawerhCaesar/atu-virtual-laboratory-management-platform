Learning checks and model boundaries

Reference run: 18 kW gradual load, 60% external SOC, 30 kW rating, 43 MJ/kg LHV, 120 s at 0.1 s. Expect approximately 0.5896 kWh generated, 157.60 g fuel, 31.32% case efficiency and 22.50 kW peak generation.

Try low SOC: 20% adds 6 kW support. Try 45 kW steady demand with a 15 kW rating: the output is capped and shortfall remains. Disable the set: after the delay and ramp response its output becomes zero.

The core applies demand clipping, SOC support, rating/enable, timestamped transport delay, ramp limiting, normalized maps and conversion equations in that order. Cumulative quantities use trapezoidal integration. The final interval is shortened only when duration is not an exact multiple of the selected step; exports record timestamps.

Demonstration maps are illustrative, not production calibration. The original SLX was not supplied and the shared conversation was inaccessible. Validation compares the supplied equations and summary reference values; no fresh full-trace Simulink comparison is claimed.

SOC is an external operating condition. Battery dynamics, emissions, motor/inverter switching, vehicle speed, wheel torque and range are outside this model. Instantaneous efficiency may be misleading during zero-power operation; use the case energy ratio for comparisons.
