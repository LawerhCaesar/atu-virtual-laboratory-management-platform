      Component key and accuracy limits
      • Silver pistons, gold connecting rods and blue crankshaft: generic slider-crank geometry, with a 0.36:1.25 crank/rod ratio in arbitrary scene units.
      • Green intake valves and orange exhaust valves: idealised stroke boundaries, with no valve overlap or variable valve timing.
      • Amber injector indicators show enabled injection and commanded pulse width; injection phasing, spray flow and fuel mass are not calculated.
      • Spark indicators position the calculated ignition advance relative to an assumed firing TDC. They are not measured ignition events.
      • The coolant marker changes with calculated control temperature; it is not a temperature field or heat-transfer solution. Fan and virtual electric-pump indicators display duty commands, not actuator RPM or flow.
      • The separate physical 1NZ setup has no electric water pump. No cylinder pressure, torque, power, fuel consumption or combustion accuracy is claimed.

      Signal guide and model boundaries

      ECT: engine coolant temperature; MAP: manifold absolute pressure; TPS: throttle position; STFT: short-term fuel trim / equivalent closed-loop fuel correction; PWM: pulse-width modulation; BTDC: before top dead centre.

      Fingerprint authorisation and voice/manual start are simulated inputs, not recognition systems. Engine RPM is a student input; the model does not simulate mechanical acceleration. Virtual ECT1, virtual ECT2, the duty-controlled fan and virtual electric water pump do not specify physical hardware.
