Educational model · safety and physical-stage limitations
This is a simplified educational model. Its equations are not Toyota 1NZ, OEM or production ECU calibration data. Do not use it to control a road vehicle or safety-critical hardware. Virtual stage only. The separate physical 1NZ setup has no electric water pump. The stages are tested independently, with no comparison.

Student inputs

Drag a slider, tap −/+ or type a value. Edited inputs are applied at the next sample; Run advances virtual time. All inputs stay within the model's accepted limits.

Scroll inside this input panel to reach the sensors and switches. Change one value, then inspect its effect on the right.

Presets reset the experiment and pause it. Run, enable ignition and authorisation, then press Start engine. A latched emergency remains set.

Emergency stays latched until explicitly cleared or Reset all. Start/stop are ten-sample virtual pulses; run or resume first.
