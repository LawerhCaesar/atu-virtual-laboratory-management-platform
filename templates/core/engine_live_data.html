Educational model · safety and physical-stage limitations
This is a simplified educational model. Its equations are not Toyota 1NZ, OEM or production ECU calibration data. Do not use it to control a road vehicle or safety-critical hardware. Virtual stage only. The separate physical 1NZ setup has no electric water pump. The stages are tested independently, with no comparison.

Student inputs

Only inputs are editable. All values are capped before calculations.

Emergency stop stays latched until Reset. Start/stop requests last 0.5 s of virtual time.

Presets change inputs only. Enable ignition and authorisation, press Start, then Run to follow the start sequence.

Reset restores all sensor and operating defaults, pauses time and clears history.

Engine operating sequence

    Time in state: 0.00 s. Prime: 2.0 s · Crank timeout: 5.0 s · Shutdown: 0.5 s.

    Signal guide and model boundaries

    ECT: engine coolant temperature; MAP: manifold absolute pressure; TPS: throttle position; STFT: short-term fuel trim / equivalent closed-loop fuel correction; PWM: pulse-width modulation; BTDC: before top dead centre.

    Fingerprint authorisation and voice/manual start are simulated inputs, not recognition systems. Engine RPM is a student input; the model does not simulate mechanical acceleration. Virtual ECT1, virtual ECT2, the duty-controlled fan and virtual electric water pump do not specify physical hardware. CSV files contain virtual demonstration history only; no assessment is automatically submitted.
