Educational model · safety and physical-stage limitations
This is a simplified educational model. Its equations are not Toyota 1NZ, OEM or production ECU calibration data. Do not use it to control a road vehicle or safety-critical hardware.     Signal guide and model boundaries

    ECT: engine coolant temperature; MAP: manifold absolute pressure; TPS: throttle position; STFT: short-term fuel trim / equivalent closed-loop fuel correction; PWM: pulse-width modulation; BTDC: before top dead centre.

    Authorisation and start requests are simulated inputs, not recognition systems. Engine RPM is a student input; the model does not simulate mechanical acceleration. Virtual ECT1, virtual ECT2, the duty-controlled fan and virtual electric water pump do not specify physical hardware.
