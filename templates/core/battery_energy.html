    Try, observe, verify

    1. Restore the reference baseline. At 0 s, confirm 50% SOC and −50.4 A charging.
    2. Run at 60×. At 1800 s, SOC should be 75% and the request switches to +100.8 A.
    3. At 3600 s, expect 25% SOC, 31.453835°C and 3601 samples. Energy received ≈9.155583 kWh; supplied ≈17.415337 kWh; loss ≈0.406426 kWh.
    4. Load High-current limit: requested −400 A is limited to −201.6 A. Load Thermal derating: −200 A is limited to −100.8 A at 40°C. Cold pack blocks charging at 0°C.
    5. Compare 1× and 60× exports: the same inputs produce identical samples; speed changes pacing only.
