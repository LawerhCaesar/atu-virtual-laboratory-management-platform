    Controller codes: 0 no intervention; 1 / 2 / 3 ABS apply / hold / release; −1 / −2 / −3 TCS restore / hold / reduce.

    Model assumptions and limits

    One equivalent wheel represents four equally loaded tyres. The model includes longitudinal motion only; steering, yaw, roll, load transfer, combined slip, hydraulic modulators and actuator delays are not modelled.

    The wheel-acceleration equation is reproduced as written in the supplied task, including its tyre-force sign. The referenced Simulink file was not supplied, and this convention and the output have not been validated against that model or a physical vehicle. Do not use for vehicle control or safety certification.

    Exports contain configuration and full-resolution results. They are local learning evidence and are not automatically saved or submitted for assessment.
