Preparing the experiment…

All-mode verification includes an artificial low-SOC control stimulus at 23–29 s. Displayed SOC remains the calculated state. Use urban, highway or manual demand for ordinary control experiments.

Engine, generator, clutch and traction drive

Representative drivetrain geometry. Wheel and locked-shaft rotation follow calculated road speed and the selected drive ratio. Free-running engine/MG1 motion is an activity cue; their RPM and clutch-slip dynamics are not calculated.

Engineering interpretation

Comparison keeps the same scenario, demand, initial state, fuel inventory and run timing, while restoring advanced model assumptions. Exports retain the full reproducible record.
