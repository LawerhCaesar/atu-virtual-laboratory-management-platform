Study assumptions, equations and verification

This is the supplied Bless passenger-van study, not a Genesis GV70 calibration. Defaults: 96s41p battery, 59.51232 kWh nominal, 45 kW fuel cell, 2 kg hydrogen, 120 kW electric motor and diesel engine. The built-in 600-second cycle is illustrative.

Electric source energy = hydrogen used × hydrogen LHV + signed battery inventory depletion. Diesel source energy = diesel litres × diesel LHV. Extra final battery energy receives no refill credit. Cost-saving comparison requires battery depletion ≥ −0.001 kWh, all three positive prices and model comparison eligibility.

Model comparison eligible means commanded distance > 0.0001 km and cumulative electric-DC and diesel unmet energy each < 0.000001 kWh. Motor shortfall and mass excess are shown separately. This is not comprehensive vehicle feasibility.

Ported from the supplied R2024b graph with 17 delayed states and 106 output channels. Numerical agreement with native traces establishes conversion fidelity, not measured vehicle validation. No detailed electrochemistry, tank pressure, achieved-speed dynamics, route-completion or structural-safety claim is made.
