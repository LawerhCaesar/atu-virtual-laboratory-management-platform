    Component key and accuracy limits

    Assumed firing order 1–3–4–2, with idealised four-stroke valve timing—not Toyota geometry or validated combustion data.

    • Silver pistons, gold connecting rods and blue crankshaft: generic slider-crank geometry, with a 0.36:1.25 crank/rod ratio in arbitrary scene units.
    • Green intake valves and orange exhaust valves: idealised stroke boundaries with smooth lift, no valve overlap or variable valve timing. The overhead shafts turn at half crank speed; their lobes are illustrative, not real cam profiles.
    • Amber injector indicators show enabled injection and commanded pulse width; injection phasing, spray flow and fuel mass are not calculated. Blue and grey moving markers show only the idealised intake/exhaust direction.
    • Spark indicators position the calculated ignition advance relative to an assumed firing TDC. They are not measured ignition events; high-speed playback uses a steady enabled indicator to avoid rapid flashing.
    • The coolant marker changes with calculated control temperature; it is not a temperature field or heat-transfer solution. Fan and virtual electric-pump readouts are duty commands; their animations use an illustrative rate, not measured RPM or flow.
    • The separate physical 1NZ setup has no electric water pump. No cylinder pressure, torque, power, fuel consumption or combustion accuracy is claimed.
