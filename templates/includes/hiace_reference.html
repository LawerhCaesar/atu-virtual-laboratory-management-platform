Common vehicle reference and model-specific assumptions

Toyota HiAce 2019 · HiAce VI (H300) · Commuter L2H2, 2.8 D-4D Automatic. The following reference specification was supplied for this study. Variant and market configurations vary; these descriptive values do not silently replace the validated model settings.

Vehicle
Diesel passenger van / commuter minibus; 10–17 seats depending on configuration; 4 doors.
Engine
1GD-FTV, 2,755 cc; front-mounted longitudinal inline four; DOHC, four valves per cylinder; common-rail injection; turbocharger and intercooler.
Rated output
163 metric hp at 3,600 rpm (approximately 120 kW); 420 N·m at 1,600–2,200 rpm.
Engine dimensions
92 × 103.6 mm bore × stroke; 15.6:1 compression ratio.
Transmission and drive
Six-speed automatic; rear-wheel drive; 70 L diesel tank.
Dimensions
5,915 × 1,950 × 2,280 mm; wheelbase 3,860 mm; front/rear overhang 950 / 1,105 mm; front/rear track 1,670–1,675 / 1,670 mm; turning diameter 12.8 m.
Mass and load
Kerb mass 2,615–2,680 kg; gross mass 3,710–3,720 kg; maximum load 1,040–1,095 kg.
Suspension
Front independent MacPherson struts, coil springs and anti-roll bar; rear rigid axle with leaf springs.
Brakes and steering
296 mm front and 320 mm rear ventilated discs; ABS; rack-and-pinion steering with hydraulic assistance.
Wheels
215/70 R16 tyres; 7J × 16 rims.
Service and additional systems
Engine oil 7.1 L; coolant 9.7 L; start–stop and diesel particulate filter.
Towing
Braked/unbraked 1,500 / 400 kg; maximum towbar vertical load 150 kg.

Actual model assumptions remain editable: 2,650 kg diesel kerb mass, 3,004 kg converted kerb mass, 540 kg shared payload, 0.35 m loaded wheel radius, 120 kW diesel and electric-machine ratings, 96s41p battery (59.51232 kWh nominal), 45 kW fuel-cell module and 2 kg initial hydrogen.
